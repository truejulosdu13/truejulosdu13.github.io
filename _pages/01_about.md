I am currently a postdoctoral scholar at [CalTech](https://www.caltech.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} in the group of [Sarah E. Reisman](http://reismangroup.caltech.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and a current member of the NSF [Center for Computer Assisted Synthesis](https://ccas.nd.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. My research focuses on the development of computational and machine learning models for organic synthesis, with the strong conviction that mechanistic understanding is the key towards building robust and data efficient models.

I studied Chemistry at the the [Ecole Normale Supérieure](https://www.chimie.ens.fr/en/chemistry-department-of-ens/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} in Paris, France. I completed my Ph. D. "Machine Learning and Mechanistic Analysis" in 2022 under the supervision of [Laurence Grimaud](https://www.chimie.ens.fr/grimaud/current-team/team-leaders/laurence-grimaud/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

Alongside with my research activities, I spent half of my Ph. D. teaching chemistry at Ecole Normale Supérieure. Organic chemistry lessons, electrochemistry tutorials and experimental sessions, most of the lesson materials are available [here](https://truejulosdu13.github.io/teaching/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.
